ceph.automation Ansible Collection — Modern Ceph Deployment

The ceph.automation Ansible Collection provides modules for Ceph cluster management compatible with ansible-core >=2.15. Works via cephadm or direct ceph binary. Legacy ceph-ansible supports through Pacific; for Quincy/Squid/Tentacle, cephadm + automation collection is the recommended path.
