Industry Updates

2 Aug 2026

EU AI Act Enforcement Begins — Storage Compliance Deadline

EU AI Act Article 10 mandates documented data governance for high-risk AI systems: data provenance, quality controls, bias analysis. Article 12 requires automatic event logging retained 6+ months. Penalties up to €35M or 7% of global turnover. "Provenance can't be retrofitted — the storage substrate has to land before the training data does."
