27 Apr 2026

Stratis 3.9.0 — In-Place Encryption & Pool Reencryption

Stratis 3.9.0 introduces in-place encryption, decryption, and reencryption of storage pools — no data migration required. Also adds cache bypass on pool start for recovering from failed cache devices. Written in Rust, layers dm, LUKS, and XFS.

Mar 2025

io_uring Zero-Copy Network Receive — Linux 6.15

Linux 6.15 added io_uring zero-copy receive, enabling DMA of packet data directly into userspace memory without kernel bounce. Benchmarks show 116.2 Gbps vs 82.2 Gbps for epoll (+41%). A 200G link saturated from a single CPU core.

Gartner: Storage Bottleneck Is #1 Constraint on AI Success

Gartner's 2026 Strategic Roadmap finds that 50%+ of organizations report data/storage bottlenecks limiting AI performance. As AI shifts from episodic training to continuous distributed inference, legacy storage architectures are the primary constraint. GPU underutilization due to storage I/O is the leading cause of stalled AI projects.

Intel Optane Discontinued — CXL Is the Future
